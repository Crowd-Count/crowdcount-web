Crowd Counter 1

Full release planned for
February 2026


Our flagship product
, soon complete and ready to hit the market. The Crowd Counter 1 is engineered to allow seamless install and use by anybody.

The Crowd Counter 1 will count the number of people entering and exiting your location,
providing you with a live count of the number of people inside.


Simply
place the small device above your door and let it do the rest.
Using its global shutter camera, it sends the footage to our powerful custom built in house NVIDIA servers for us to do the processing. That way, the device can remain inexpensive and reliable for our customers.

Integrated with our wonderful
iOS App
which allows you to access this information from anywhere and anytime. The app also serves as a tool to help you set it up for the first time, and add multiple if you have multiple entrances. If you would like to manage your existing counter on desktop or without the mobile app, we also have a web interface at your disposal.
