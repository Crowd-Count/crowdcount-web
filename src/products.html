Crowd Counter 1


Our flagship product
, complete and available to anyone. The Crowd Counter 1 is engineered to allow seamless install and use by anybody.

The Crowd Counter 1 counts the number of people entering and exiting your location,
providing you with a live count of the number of people inside and the amount of entries.


Simply
place the small device above your door and let it do the rest.
Using its camera, it sends the footage to our powerful custom built in house NVIDIA servers (where we have consistently reached
99% accuracy
) for us to do the processing. That way, the device can remain inexpensive and reliable for our customers. In addition, we restrict our own access to the footage, ensuring your privacy is maintained at all times.

Integrated with our wonderful iOS App which allows you to access this information from anywhere and anytime. The app also serves as a tool to help you set it up for the first time, and add multiple if you have multiple entrances. If you would like to manage your existing counter on desktop or without the mobile app, we also have a web interface at your disposal.
